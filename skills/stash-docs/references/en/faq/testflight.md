# TestFlight Related Issues

Stash conducts grayscale testing by distributing beta versions through TestFlight. All purchasing users can apply to participate in TestFlight testing. Inside **Stash installed from the App Store**, you can enter your email in "Settings - More Settings" to receive invitations sent out by TestFlight.

> [!NOTE]
> Before installing the TestFlight version, please go to "Tools - System
> Information" (Stash V2) or "Settings - Diagnostics" (Stash V1) to confirm the
> valid status of the current device authorization. Due to Apple's sandbox
> restrictions, device activation cannot currently be done in the TestFlight
> version.

- Due to Apple's restrictions, TestFlight allows a maximum of 10,000 accounts to participate. Stash Network Ltd. reserves the right to remove inactive accounts.
- The Apple ID applying for TestFlight can be different from the purchasing account, but each purchasing account can only have one TestFlight slot at a time. When reapplying, the old slot will be removed.
- Since Stash is not available in the Chinese region, please do not use a Chinese region Apple ID to apply for TestFlight. Using a Chinese region Apple ID may encounter the issue of "The requested app is not available or does not exist."
- TestFlight belongs to the nature of beta (testing) versions. While experiencing new features, there may be bugs or unstable situations. Please switch to the App Store version or TestFlight test version based on your actual situation.
