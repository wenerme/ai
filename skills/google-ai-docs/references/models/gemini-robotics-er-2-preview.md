<br />

Gemini Robotics ER 2 is a vision-language model (VLM) for robotics that
accepts text, image, video, and audio input. It supports spatial reasoning,
video understanding, agentic code execution, multi-step tool
orchestration, and multi-robot coordination.
[Try in Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/prompts/new_chat?model=gemini-robotics-er-2-preview)

## Documentation

Visit the [Robotics](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/robotics-overview) page for full coverage
of features and capabilities.
