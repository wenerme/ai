# FAQ

Last updated Oct 15, 2026|Copy as Markdown| [View as Markdown](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/reference/faq/index.md)| [Agent setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agent-setup/)

Find direct answers to common Cloudflare Email Service questions.

## What happens if I exceed my sending limits?

Email Service may queue or reject emails that exceed sending limits. Error responses include rate-limit information.

Refer to [Email Service limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/limits/) for quota details and increase requests.

## What affects my sender reputation?

Sender reputation measures how much inbox providers trust your email. Authentication, bounces, complaints, volume patterns, engagement, and sending history affect it.

Refer to [Deliverability](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/concepts/deliverability/) for related reputation factors.

## Can I use Email Service for marketing emails?

No. Email Service supports transactional email only.

## How do I report email abuse or spam?

Report email abuse to [mailabuse@cloudflare.com](mailto:mailabuse@cloudflare.com). Include:

- Full email headers
- Description of the issue
- Any relevant account information

Refer to [Postmaster contact information](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/reference/postmaster/#contact-information) for the canonical address.
