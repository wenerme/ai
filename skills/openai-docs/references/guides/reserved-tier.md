# Reserved Tier

> For the complete documentation index, see [llms.txt](/llms.txt). Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to the page URL.

Reserved Tier gives Enterprise customers guaranteed API capacity for a specific model, up to the amount they reserve in dollars per minute. When demand for that model is high, OpenAI prioritizes requests within your reservation over pay-as-you-go traffic on the same shared infrastructure. Use Reserved Tier when you need predictable capacity for a production workload or expect a traffic spike during a product launch or special event, such as Black Friday or Cyber Monday.

Your reservation automatically covers eligible Standard and Fast mode requests for that model. You pay for the capacity you reserve, even when you use less. Usage above your reservation is billed at your usual pay-as-you-go rates and is subject to your usual rate limits.

Reserved Tier is available on select Astra, Sol, Terra, and Luna models. To get access, [contact sales](https://openai.com/contact-sales/). If your organization already has access, your authorized purchaser can see the models available to reserve by selecting **Add capacity** in [Capacity Management](https://platform.openai.com/settings/organization/capacity-management/overview).
