**Qwen-Image-Edit-2511** is an enhanced version of Qwen-Image-Edit-2509, featuring multiple improvements including notably better consistency. This model extends Qwen-Image's unique text rendering capabilities to editing tasks, enabling precise text editing with dual semantic and appearance editing capabilities.

**Key Enhancements in Qwen-Image-Edit-2511**:
- **Mitigate Image Drift**: Improved stability during editing operations
- **Improved Character Consistency**: Better preservation of identity and visual characteristics during imaginative edits
- **Multi-Person Consistency**: High-fidelity fusion of multiple person images into coherent group shots
- **Integrated LoRA Capabilities**: Popular community LoRAs built directly into the base model
- **Enhanced Industrial Design Generation**: Better support for batch industrial product design and material replacement
- **Strengthened Geometric Reasoning**: Direct generation of auxiliary construction lines for design or annotation

### Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 Multiple Angles with the 3D Camera Node {#template_qwen_multiangle_app}

Load one photo, orbit a 3D camera around the subject, and Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 renders that same subject from the angle you picked. The template pairs the edit model with the multiple-angles LoRA, so you can re-shoot one input image from any angle you pick.

#### Camera Angle controls

The **Compose Camera Angle Prompt** node adds a 3D viewport above its angle fields. Pick a viewpoint in the viewport and the node writes a shot description such as `front view eye-level shot medium shot` into the workflow prompt, so you do not type it by hand. The angle fields follow as you move.

- **Camera / Object**: **Camera** moves you around the subject, **Object** looks through the lens and turns the subject instead.
- **Horizontal angle, Vertical angle, Shot size**: named positions: eight around the subject from `Front` to `Front left`, four elevations from `Low angle` to `High angle`, and three shot sizes from `Wide shot` to `Close-up`. Dragging in the 3D viewport sets the same values.
- **Preview**: adds a picture-in-picture window with what the camera frame contains.

Connect an image to the node's optional image input to show it on the front face of the subject, which makes left and right obvious while you orbit.

Turbo mode (`enable_turbo_mode`, a control on the subgraph node) is on by default: 4 steps, CFG 1 and the 4-step Lightning LoRA. Switch it off for the 40-step, CFG 4 pass without the Lightning LoRA.
