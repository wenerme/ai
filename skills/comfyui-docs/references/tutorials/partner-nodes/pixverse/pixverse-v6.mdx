**PixVerse V6** generates video from a text prompt, animates a still image, or composes a scene from reference subjects and backgrounds. **PixVerse V6 Max** is the higher-fidelity tier of the same family and appears as a model option in the text to video and image to video nodes.

ComfyUI runs the family through partner nodes: **PixVerse V6 Text to Video**, **PixVerse V6 Image to Video**, and **PixVerse V6 Fusion (Reference to Video)**. They are cloud API nodes, so they need a Comfy account with credits and download no model files locally.

Clips run from 1 to 15 seconds at 360p, 540p, 720p, or 1080p, with a native audio track generated by default. PixVerse V6 adds style presets and multi-shot cutting inside one generation. V6 Max renders at higher fidelity and drops the style presets. PixVerse records the seed but does not reproduce a run from it.

## Available workflows

### Text to Video

Describe the shot and the model renders one clip, with native audio if you leave it enabled. This is the mode with no input files at all.

**Example prompts**

- `Single shot, low-angle tracking across wet asphalt at night, a courier runs past neon signs, shallow depth of field, cool blue grade`
- `Slow orbit around a clay teapot on a wooden table, morning window light, dust in the air, macro detail`
- `Two dancers in a bright studio, handheld camera, fast footwork, natural sound of footsteps and breathing`

### Reference to Video (Fusion)

Place your own subjects into your own scene. Connect images as subjects or backgrounds and refer to each one by its slot name in the prompt, for example `@Subject1 walks through @Background1`. Fusion runs on PixVerse V6 only. Connecting a reference video switches the node to Omni mode: the output length then matches the longest reference video, and the video can also lend motion, framing, or style.

## Node controls

| Control | Values | Applies to | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `model` | `PixVerse V6`, `PixVerse V6 Max` | text to video and image to video (both tiers), Fusion (V6 only) | Model tier and the settings that tier exposes |
| `prompt` | text, up to 5000 characters | all V6 nodes | The shot description: subject, action, camera, light |
| `negative_prompt` | text, up to 2048 characters | all V6 nodes | Elements to keep out of the frame |
| `quality` | `360p`, `540p`, `720p` (default), `1080p` | all V6 nodes | Output resolution tier. Frame size also depends on the aspect ratio |
| `aspect_ratio` | `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16`, `2:3`, `3:2`, `21:9`, and `auto` on Fusion | text to video, Fusion | Frame shape. `auto` needs a reference video |
| `duration_seconds` | 1 to 15, default 5 | all V6 nodes | Clip length |
| `generate_audio` | on by default | all V6 nodes | Render a native audio track with the video |
| `multi_clip` | off by default | text to video (both tiers), image to video on V6 | Let the model cut the clip into several shots |
| `style` | `none`, `anime`, `3d_animation`, `clay`, `comic`, `cyberpunk`, `realistic` | V6 only | Look applied to the whole clip. Not available on V6 Max |
| `seed` | integer | all V6 nodes | Recorded by PixVerse but not used to reproduce a run |
| `subjects` | up to 8 images | Fusion | People or objects placed into the scene as `@Subject1` to `@Subject8` |
| `backgrounds` | up to 2 images | Fusion | Scenes the subjects are placed into as `@Background1`, `@Background2` |
| `videos` | up to 2, 15 seconds in total | Fusion | Reference footage read as `@Video1`, `@Video2`. Also switches on Omni mode |

Fusion accepts up to 7 reference images when no reference video is connected and up to 10 with one connected. Image to video keeps the aspect ratio of the input image, so `aspect_ratio` is not offered there.

## Prompting tips

- **Write the shot, not the mood.** One paragraph with subject, action, camera move, and light gives the model something to render: `low-angle tracking shot, a courier runs past neon signs, cool blue grade`. A list of adjectives alone usually drifts.
- **Describe audio when you keep it on.** Footsteps, impacts, ambience, and a music style all land in the generated track. Turn `generate_audio` off when you plan to add your own sound.
- **Use `multi_clip` for a sequence.** It is how a single generation returns several shots with a consistent look, and the prompt should then number the shots.
- **Label every reference in Fusion.** A connected subject or background that the prompt never mentions is usually ignored, so write `@Subject1` and `@Background1` where they belong in the action.
- **Keep negatives short and concrete.** `text, watermark, logo, subtitles, oversaturated colors` works better than a long list of style words.
- **Do not chase seed reproducibility.** The seed is stored for reference only, so a re-run with the same seed is a new generation.
